# Code of Conduct

Jiaojie welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participation must remain constructive, evidence-oriented, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and results without attacking people;
- distinguish facts, measurements, inference, and opinion;
- disclose limitations and conflicts of interest;
- protect private data and report security issues through the private channel;
- credit prior work and respect licenses;
- accept that a failed reproduction is useful project evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate misrepresentation of test evidence, secret disclosure, license evasion, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when sensitive, and open to a concise appeal.

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private repository contact or private vulnerability-reporting channel. Do not publish another person's sensitive details while reporting.
