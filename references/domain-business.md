# Business domain guidance

Use this reference for strategy, operations, planning, commercial analysis, or organizational decisions.
Apply the core record model unchanged; this file adds capture priorities and checks, not wire fields.

## Capture priorities

- Capture the business objective, decision context, scope, horizon, success criteria, and non-goals.
- Record stakeholders, decision owners, reviewers, affected parties, and the authority each held at capture time.
- Preserve metric definitions, units, currency, period, denominator, data source, and calculation basis.
- Separate observed performance, reported figures, forecasts, scenarios, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Record strategic decisions, rejected options, failed experiments, dependencies, and reversal triggers.
- Preserve customer, market, operational, legal, security, and reputational constraints.
- Capture risks, likelihood evidence, impact, mitigations, owners, and invalidation conditions.
- Record unresolved approvals, negotiations, commitments, and decisions that remain genuinely open.
- Preserve spreadsheets, dashboards, contracts, diagrams, presentations, and signed documents as artifacts.
- Record the next analysis, approval, negotiation, experiment, or execution action and its completion criteria.

## Principals, time, and external state

- Attribute statements to executives, employees, customers, advisers, agents, tools, and external sources separately.
- Preserve conflicting stakeholder or agent positions without inventing consensus or authority.
- Attach observation and validity times to prices, forecasts, budgets, policies, staffing, and market conditions.
- Treat CRM, finance, inventory, contracts, market feeds, approvals, and operational systems as external state.
- Revalidate volatile numbers, commitments, counterparties, and regulatory facts before acting.
- Preserve non-text evidence such as financial models, signed PDFs, decks, recordings, and dashboard exports.

## Action and authorization checks

- Use the action graph for analysis, review, approval, contracting, communication, execution, and rollback.
- Place approval, compliance, funding, and external-state checks before affected execution actions.
- Do not infer authority from seniority, historical participation, copied recipients, or an earlier handoff.
- Require current authorization for contracts, payments, hiring, pricing, disclosure, outreach, or system changes.
- Never treat a forecast, recommendation, or scenario as an approved commitment.
- Keep credentials, personal data, confidential terms, and regulated information within the approved boundary.

## Handoff checks

- Verify that every number retains its unit, currency, period, source, and epistemic basis.
- Verify that current decisions and rejected options remain distinguishable.
- Verify that stakeholder authority is captured historically but revalidated for present actions.
- Verify that critical spreadsheets or contracts are embedded or honestly marked unavailable.
- Verify that external operational state and market data carry freshness requirements.
- Verify that risks and invalidation conditions remain connected to the affected decision or action.
- Verify that competing stakeholder branches are not flattened by last-write-wins behavior.
- Block high-risk execution until approval, authorization, and required external-state checks pass.
