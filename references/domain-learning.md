# Learning domain guidance

Use this reference for tutoring, study planning, skill acquisition, practice, or assessment preparation.
Apply the core record model unchanged; this file adds capture priorities and checks, not wire fields.

## Capture priorities

- Capture the learner's goal, syllabus or competency boundary, target date, and success criteria.
- Record the learner's demonstrated baseline separately from self-reported confidence or inferred ability.
- Preserve mastered concepts, partial understanding, misconceptions, recurring errors, and open questions.
- Connect exercises, answers, feedback, and corrections to the concepts they demonstrate.
- Record accommodations, preferred explanation style, language, pace, and practice format with their authority.
- Preserve prior teaching approaches that helped, failed, confused, or were explicitly rejected.
- Capture assessment constraints, allowed materials, scoring rules, and deadlines when relevant.
- Record spaced-practice or review commitments and the evidence used to schedule them.
- Preserve the next concept, exercise, review, or assessment action and all prerequisites.
- Keep exemplar answers distinct from the learner's own work and from evaluator judgments.

## Principals, time, and external state

- Attribute statements to learner, tutor, teacher, examiner, agent, grader, or external source.
- Keep conflicting feedback from multiple teachers or agents explicit until resolved.
- Attach dates to assessments, study plans, deadlines, curriculum versions, and time-sensitive requirements.
- Treat learning-platform progress, course availability, exam rules, and enrollment status as external state.
- Revalidate mutable schedules, policies, and platform progress before relying on them.
- Preserve handwriting images, diagrams, flashcards, audio pronunciation, and recorded practice as non-text artifacts.

## Action and authorization checks

- Use the action graph for prerequisite concepts, parallel practice, reviews, and assessment milestones.
- Do not mark a concept mastered solely because an agent explained it or the learner expressed confidence.
- Block progression only when the missing prerequisite materially prevents the selected next action.
- Do not infer permission to submit work, enroll, purchase materials, message instructors, or alter grades.
- Require current authorization for platform submissions, bookings, payments, sharing, or account changes.
- Keep health, disability, identity, and educational records within the declared sensitive boundary.

## Handoff checks

- Verify that demonstrated evidence supports each mastery or misconception claim.
- Verify that answered questions will not be asked again unless a scheduled reassessment is justified.
- Verify that learner work, model examples, and external answer keys remain distinguishable.
- Verify that the next exercise matches current level, prerequisites, and the learner's active goal.
- Verify that old misconceptions are not revived as current beliefs after they were corrected.
- Verify that deadlines and exam rules carry freshness and source evidence.
- Verify that required non-text work is embedded or honestly marked unavailable.
- Block high-stakes educational actions when identity, rules, or authorization must be revalidated.
